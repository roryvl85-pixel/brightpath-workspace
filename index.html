Why Signal & Script

I'm Rory, founder of Signal & Script. Before building automated workflows, I spent nearly a decade as an ISO 9001:2015-certified auditor in engineering, evaluating how companies' systems and processes connect — how one process's output becomes the trigger for the next input, so a system runs as a continuous, verified chain instead of disconnected steps. That's precisely the discipline behind good workflow automation: I'm not just connecting apps, I'm applying the same process-integrity thinking I used for a decade in formal audits.

On the content side, Signal & Script's writers focus on the same principle applied to communication: making sure your message actually connects with the people reading it, consistently, in your voice.
