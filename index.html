Hi, I'm Rory — I built BrightPath because I'm a dad first, and an engineer second.

A few years ago I was deep into a high-pressure project — long hours, early mornings, the kind of work that follows you home even when you're not there. One evening I called my son, just to check in. He was struggling with something from his textbook, and I could hear the frustration in his voice. I wanted so badly to help him. But I was hundreds of kilometres away, exhausted, and there was simply nobody there who could explain it to him the way he needed it explained. That moment — of wanting to be there for your child and not being able to — is what started all of this.

Lumi isn't trying to replace teachers or parents. It's trying to be the patient voice in the room at 8pm on a Tuesday, when homework isn't going well and everyone's tired. Every feature on this site — the reports that land in your inbox, the way Lumi remembers your child's curriculum and learning style, even the smallest details — comes from asking "would this actually help a parent like me?"

Thank you for trusting us with your child's learning. I read every message that comes through this site personally.

Rory Lodewick Founder, BrightPath

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

No installations. No complicated setup. Your child can be learning with Lumi today.

1

Register online

Fill in a short form about your child — their grade, subjects, curriculum, and a few personal details so Lumi can connect with them.

2

Receive your link

We email you a personal session link. No app to download. Works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer.

3

Start learning

Your child taps the link and starts talking to Lumi. Natural voice conversation — just like speaking to a tutor.
